How it works

Four steps from a real room to a shippable plan.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Photos or a blueprint. Desktop, or QR to your phone.

    Walk the room with your phone or drop in a floor plan. We reconstruct the shell — walls, openings, ceiling height — as integer millimetres, then let you nudge any measurement you know better than we do. Nothing downstream is guessed.

    • 12-photo sweep, or a single dimensioned plan PDF
    • Desktop session hands off to phone by QR, live
    • Every wall length editable before you commit
  2. 02

    Bring your furniture

    Upload what you own, or pick what actually exists.

    Photograph the sofa you're not throwing away and we measure it into the same millimetre space. Or browse a catalog of real products whose depth, width and height come from the manufacturer, not from a language model's imagination.

    • Own-furniture capture → dimensioned proxy solid
    • Catalog items carry SKU, dims and clearance rules
    • Duplicate detection so your sofa exists once
  3. 03

    Arrange

    Let the AI propose. Or move it yourself, in 3D.

    The solver returns several candidate layouts you can re-roll, each scored on circulation width, sightlines and door swing. Don't like any of them? Drag pieces in the 3D editor with live collision and clearance feedback. It will tell you when the coffee table is 40mm too close, and it will not pretend otherwise.

    • Re-roll for four fresh, scored candidates
    • Hard violations, not silent overlaps
    • Snap to walls, respect swing arcs and walkways
  4. 04

    Ship it

    An annotated blueprint someone can build from.

    Export a plan sheet with named furniture, dimension chains and an FF&E schedule, plus rendered views for the client. Hand it to a contractor, a stager or a landlord and they can act on it without asking you a single follow-up question.

    • Dimension chains from wall face to item edge
    • FF&E schedule with SKUs and quantities
    • Rendered views from the same true geometry

What we actually promise

Four commitments, stated plainly.

Everything is stored as integer millimetres — room shell, openings, furniture, offsets. We never round to make a layout work. If your 2180mm sofa does not fit a 2100mm alcove, you get a violation with the exact overlap, not a smaller sofa.

The layout solver treats circulation width, door swing arcs, drawer pull-out and seated sightlines as constraints — not suggestions. Candidates that break them are scored down or rejected outright, and you can see which rule failed and by how much.

Most people are not starting from an empty room. Photograph what you own, we measure it into the same coordinate space as the catalog, and the solver plans around it. The inherited armchair is a first-class object, not something to design away.

A plan sheet with named furniture, dimension chains referenced from wall faces, a scale bar and an FF&E schedule with SKUs. Plus rendered views generated from the same geometry, so the picture and the plan can never disagree.
